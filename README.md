## 🌌 About IC-Orbit

IC-Orbit brings together students passionate about coding, software development, technology, and innovation.  
We create a collaborative environment where members learn from each other, build practical projects, explore open source, and turn ideas into working solutions.

---

## 🛰️ What We Do

- 💡 **Programming & Problem-Solving** — practice logic, strengthen fundamentals, and solve real challenges.
- 🌐 **Web Development & Software Engineering** — design and build modern applications across the stack.
- 🧩 **Collaborative Student Projects** — work in teams and convert ideas into usable products.
- 🌍 **Open-Source Contributions** — learn contribution workflows and participate in community-driven development.
- 📚 **Peer Learning & Knowledge Sharing** — exchange insights, resources, and best practices.
- 🔭 **Emerging Technology Exploration** — stay curious about evolving tools, platforms, and trends.
